# Denver voter guide, November 3, 2026

This folder is a decision aid for a voter in the City and County of Denver. It does not recommend how to vote.

Start with [ballot-summary.md](ballot-summary.md). That file is a table of every contest that appears on Denver’s combined sample ballot, with polling where a public survey was found, and a link to a longer guide for each choice.

The longer guides are in [guides/](guides/). Each one separates:

- official ballot language and nonpartisan staff analysis
- comments submitted for and against, which election offices do not always verify
- news reports
- polls, which are labeled by date and geography
- priorities that can move a yes/no or candidate choice either way

The included [2026 Ballot Information Booklet](2026-eng-ballot-info-book_accessible.pdf) covers only the five Denver measures that do not raise taxes: Referred Questions 2H, 2I, 2J, and 2K, and Initiated Ordinance 311. Statewide measures are analyzed in the [2026 Colorado Blue Book](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/sites/default/files/2026-bluebook-english-accessible_1.pdf). Candidate lists are from the [Denver Elections Division](https://www.denvergov.org/Government/Agencies-Departments-Offices/Agencies-Departments-Offices-Directory/Denver-Clerk-and-Recorder/Elections-Division/Upcoming-Elections).

Your personal ballot is smaller than the sample. State legislative districts, congressional district (1 or 6), the University of Colorado regent race, the State Board of Education race, and RTD districts depend on address. Look up the ballot at [GoVoteColorado.gov](https://www.govotecolorado.gov).

Ballots must be in the Clerk and Recorder’s possession by 7 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
