## What can swing a vote

No candidate questionnaire was compiled, so this guide does not assign any of them a position on Amendment 87, Proposition NN, or Proposition 137. Those measures change how much revenue the state keeps. The treasurer does not set tax rates. The legislature and the measures themselves do.

| If this is the priority | What to weigh |
| --- | --- |
| Party control of a low-profile statewide office | Bridges is the Democratic nominee and Grantham is the Republican nominee. |
| A non-major-party treasurer | Barr or Sturgis. Their investment or unclaimed-property positions were not sourced. |
| Using this race as a proxy for the tax measures | It is a weak proxy. Vote the tax measures directly. They are separate questions. |
