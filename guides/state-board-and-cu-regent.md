# State Board of Education and CU Regent

These two contests are easy to mix up because both follow congressional-district lines, and Denver sits in more than one district. The city’s combined sample ballot prints every contest that any Denver voter might see. You will see one education contest, one regent contest, or possibly only one of the two, depending on address.

## State Board of Education, Congressional District 1

Printed on the Denver candidate list. Voters in District 1.

| Candidate | Party |
| --- | --- |
| Lisa Escárcega | Democratic |
| Frederick Espinoza | Republican |
| Clayton Casciato | Libertarian |

The State Board sets statewide K-12 policy. Proposition 134 would give the **commissioner of education**, who works with the board’s system, a duty to enforce the sports-designation rule against public school districts. Voting for a board member does not enact Proposition 134. It chooses who sits on the board that would govern alongside that rule if voters pass it.

No public poll of this race was located. Platforms were not compiled.

| If this is the priority | It points toward |
| --- | --- |
| The Democratic nominee in District 1 | Escárcega |
| The Republican nominee | Espinoza |
| The Libertarian nominee | Casciato |

District 6 voters should not assume this trio is on their ballot. The Denver candidate list provided for this guide names a District 1 State Board race and does not name a District 6 State Board race.

## Regent of the University of Colorado, Congressional District 6

Printed on the Denver sample ballot. Voters in the Denver portion of District 6.

| Candidate | Party |
| --- | --- |
| Veanessa Burbage | Republican |
| Ilana Spiegel | Democratic |
| Joe Johnson | Libertarian |

Regents govern the University of Colorado system, including the Denver campus. Proposition 134 would also cover collegiate teams. A regent does not, by winning this seat, decide that statewide ballot question.

No public poll was located. Platforms were not compiled.

| If this is the priority | It points toward |
| --- | --- |
| The Democratic nominee | Spiegel |
| The Republican nominee | Burbage |
| The Libertarian nominee | Johnson |

The Denver Elections Division’s candidate table, as retrieved for this guide, lists the District 6 regent race and does not list a District 1 regent race. If your ballot shows a different education or regent contest, the ballot controls.
