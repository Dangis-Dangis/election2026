# RTD Board of Directors

The Regional Transportation District board is nonpartisan. Denver voters are in **District B** or **District C**, not both. The sample ballot prints both.

[Back to the summary](../ballot-summary.md)

## District B

Vote for one:

- John Glenn
- Bianka Emerson

## District C

- Joe Meyer

No other District C candidate appears on the Denver sample ballot.

## What this race is, and is not

RTD runs the existing bus and rail system. Ballot Issue 7A is a different government: the Front Range Passenger Rail District’s proposed sales tax for Colorado Connector intercity rail. A vote for an RTD director does not pass or defeat 7A. The “against” comments on 7A do argue that new rail could compete with existing transit. That is an argument about 7A, not a finding that either RTD candidate supports or opposes the tax.
