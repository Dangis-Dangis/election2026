# Colorado Senate and House districts in Denver

Each Denver voter has one state Senate contest and one state House contest. The combined sample ballot prints all of them. Precinct, not preference, decides which pair you see.

[Back to the summary](../ballot-summary.md)

Names and parties are from the Denver Elections Division’s 2026 candidate list and the English/Spanish sample ballot. No public poll of any of these districts was located. Issue positions were not compiled. Inventing a platform from party would overstate what this project checked.

These seats matter beyond the names. The legislature writes the budget that would spend Amendment 87’s new revenue, Proposition NN’s Children’s Account, and Proposition 137’s transfers. It would also be the body the Blue Book says may have to resolve a conflict if both Amendment 87 and Proposition 136 pass.

## What can swing a vote

| If this is the priority | What to weigh |
| --- | --- |
| Party control of the chamber that will implement the tax and criminal measures | The Democratic or Republican nominee in your district. Unopposed District 31 does not offer a party choice. |
| A non-major-party option where one exists | House 4 (Forward), House 7 (Libertarian), House 8 (Unity), Senate 34 (Libertarian). |
| A candidate’s vote on a specific bill | Not in this guide. Look up the incumbent’s legislative record, or the challenger’s own questionnaire, before treating party as a promise on NN, 87, or 132. |
