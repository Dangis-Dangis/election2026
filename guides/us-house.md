# U.S. House

Denver is split. Most of the city is **Congressional District 1**. A portion is **Congressional District 6**. The sample ballot prints both. Only one is on your ballot. Check GoVoteColorado.gov.

[Back to the summary](../ballot-summary.md)

## District 1

Ballot order on the Denver sample ballot:

| Candidate | Party |
| --- | --- |
| Melat Kiros | Democratic |
| Christy Peterson | Republican |
| Critter Milton | Unity |
| Chad Humphrey | Libertarian |
| Shimon Blau | Unaffiliated |

Write-ins listed by the elections division: Kevin StClair (Democratic), John Wren (Democratic), Stephen Replin (unaffiliated).

This seat is open for the general election. Incumbent Diana DeGette lost the Democratic primary on June 30, 2026. Certified results reported by Ballotpedia, matching the Decision Desk HQ count: **Melat Kiros 83,855 (53.2 percent), Diana DeGette 62,715 (39.8 percent), Wanda James 11,075 (7.0 percent)**, 157,645 votes. NBC News and Politico described Kiros as a democratic socialist, a lawyer who immigrated from Ethiopia as a child, endorsed by Bernie Sanders, the Democratic Socialists of America, and Justice Democrats. They described the campaign contrast as urgency with Democrats out of power in Washington, against DeGette’s record, including her role as a 2021 impeachment manager. Those are news characterizations of the primary, not a general-election platform.

Christy Peterson is the Republican nominee. This guide did not compile her platform or Kiros’s general-election platform beyond the primary coverage.

A website titled electionresults.app displayed “final” District 1 general-election percentages in late October 2026. Election Day is November 3. Those figures are not official results and are not used here.

### Polling

No public general-election poll of District 1 was located.

### What can swing a District 1 vote

| If this is the priority | It points toward |
| --- | --- |
| The Democrat who won the primary on an insurgent, democratic-socialist platform as described by NBC and Politico | Kiros, if that description matches the representation you want. Read her own general-election materials before treating a primary headline as the platform. |
| A Republican alternative to that nominee | Peterson |
| A non-major-party candidate | Milton, Humphrey, or Blau. Their platforms were not compiled. |
| Keeping DeGette | She is not on the general-election ballot. |
