# Secretary of State

**On every Denver ballot.** Vote for one. The office runs elections, business filings, and campaign finance.

[Back to the summary](../ballot-summary.md)

Jena Griswold, the current secretary of state, is the Democratic nominee for attorney general, not for this office. She certified the 2026 ballot on September 4, 2026.

## What can swing a vote

Candidate questionnaires were not compiled. The priorities that belong to the office, rather than to a platform this guide did not verify, are:

| If this is the priority | What to weigh |
| --- | --- |
| How mail ballots are run if Amendment 84 passes | The next secretary would implement a new ID-number check, cure process, and voter-notification system. The Blue Book assigns much of the cost to counties, with state reimbursement and a state outreach campaign. |
| Continuity of the current mail-and-drop-box system if Amendment 84 fails | The office still writes rules, maintains the voter file, and reimburses counties under existing law. |
| A party change in the office that oversees elections | Gonzalez is the Democratic nominee. Wiley is the Republican nominee. The other three candidates are the non-major-party choices on the ballot. Their election-administration positions were not sourced here. |

Do not infer a candidate’s view of Amendment 84 from party alone. That inference is not in the sources used for this guide.
